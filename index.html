How to play

  • Each side has five tank units standing in straight lines on a 10 × 10 field: a T-34 column (5 tanks), a Sherman company (4), a Churchill troop (3), a Tiger platoon (3) and a Renault FT section (2).
  • Place your units, then take turns. On your turn pick a cell on the enemy field and fire. You are told whether you hit a tank, and when a whole unit is destroyed.
  • Rocks and lakes are the same on both fields. Nothing can stand there and they cannot be fired at.
  • Once per battle you may call an air strike, which bombs three cells in a row, and send a scout drone, which reports how many intact tanks stand in a 3 × 3 area.
  • The first to destroy all five enemy units wins.

Two devices

The devices talk with short chirps from speaker to microphone, so they must be close together and reasonably undisturbed. No internet connection is used for the game itself. After the battle each device checks that the other one reported honestly.

Two devices, linked by sound

  1. Open this page on both devices and choose this option on both.
  2. Turn the volume up. On a phone, switch silent mode off.
  3. Put the devices close together. Two phones work best top to top, a few centimetres apart.
  4. Tap Call on one device. The other answers by itself.
